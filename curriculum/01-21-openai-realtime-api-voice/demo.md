# OpenAI Realtime API — Voice Interfaces
## Demonstrate

### Lesson Duration
15 minutes

---

## Demo Objective

The instructor will demonstrate how to:

- Establish a realtime audio connection.
- Stream voice input.
- Receive live AI responses.
- Handle interruptions and delays.

---

## Demo Setup

Requirements:

- OpenAI Realtime API access.
- Microphone input.
- WebSocket or streaming client.
- Stable internet connection.

---

## Instructor Walkthrough

### Step 1: Connection Setup

The instructor will:
- Open a realtime session.
- Authenticate securely.
- Confirm audio streaming readiness.

---

### Step 2: Live Interaction

The instructor will:
- Speak to the system.
- Receive partial transcriptions.
- Hear AI responses in near real time.

---

### Step 3: Interruption Handling

The instructor will:
- Interrupt mid-response.
- Observe system behavior.
- Discuss conversational flow.

---

## Demo Observations

- Latency shapes perception.
- Partial results improve experience.
- Errors are immediately visible.

---

## Demo Takeaways

- Realtime feels personal.
- Small delays matter.
- Robust handling is required.
