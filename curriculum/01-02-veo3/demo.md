# Veo3 — Text-to-Video Generation
## Demonstrate

### Lesson Duration
15 minutes

---

## Demo Objective

The instructor will demonstrate how to:

- Write effective text-to-video prompts.
- Generate short video clips using Veo3.
- Control style, pacing, and camera instructions.
- Compare multiple outputs from similar prompts.

---

## Demo Setup

Requirements:

- Access to Veo3.
- Understanding of structured prompting.
- Sample marketing scenarios.

---

## Instructor Walkthrough

### Step 1: Basic Prompt

The instructor will start with a simple prompt:
- Single scene.
- Minimal instructions.

Students will observe:
- Visual ambiguity.
- Generic motion.
- Lack of focus.

---

### Step 2: Structured Prompt

The instructor will refine the prompt to include:
- Scene description.
- Camera movement.
- Visual tone.
- Intended audience.

Students will compare results side by side.

---

### Step 3: Ad-Oriented Prompt

The instructor will demonstrate:
- Short runtime constraints.
- Product-focused framing.
- Clear visual storytelling.

Discussion:
- Why ads require different prompts than art.

---

## Demo Observations

- Video quality improves with specificity.
- Motion instructions matter.
- Short prompts waste model capacity.

---

## Demo Takeaways

- Text-to-video prompting is a skill.
- Structure beats creativity alone.
- Expect variation, not consistency.
