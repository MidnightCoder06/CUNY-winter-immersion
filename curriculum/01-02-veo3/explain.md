# Veo3 — Text-to-Video Generation
## Explain

### Lesson Duration
10–15 minutes

---

## Terminal Objectives (SWBAT)

By the end of this section, students will be able to:

- Explain what Veo3 is and how it differs from image and text models.
- Describe how text-to-video models work at a high level.
- Identify business and marketing use cases for AI-generated video.
- Explain the limitations and risks of generative video.

---

## What Is Veo3?

Veo3 is Google’s text-to-video generation model.  
It transforms written prompts into short video clips.

Think of Veo3 as a **virtual film crew**.  
You describe the scene.  
It handles lighting, motion, framing, and pacing.

---

## What Does It Do?

Veo3 can:

- Generate short video clips from text prompts.
- Interpret camera angles, motion, and scene descriptions.
- Apply visual styles such as cinematic, documentary, or ad-focused.
- Produce multiple variations from the same concept.

It does not replace professional video production.  
It accelerates ideation and iteration.

---

## Why Is It Used?

Veo3 is used because:

- Video ads outperform static ads.
- Traditional video production is slow and expensive.
- Marketers need rapid iteration.
- Early-stage teams need visuals without studios.

This is not about perfection.  
This is about **speed to signal**.

---

## Common Use Cases

- Short-form ad creatives.
- Social media video experiments.
- Concept visualization.
- Storyboarding for larger productions.
- Rapid creative testing.

---

## Mental Model

Text → Image models create moments.  
Text → Video models create **movement and narrative**.

You are no longer prompting an object.  
You are prompting **time**.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Veo3 turns ideas into motion.
- Prompt clarity controls visual quality.
- Video generation is probabilistic, not deterministic.
- This tool shines in iteration, not polish.
