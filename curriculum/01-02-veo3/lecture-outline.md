# Veo3 — Text-to-Video Generation
## Explain


---

## Demo Objective

The instructor will demonstrate how to:

- Write effective text-to-video prompts.
- Generate short video clips using Veo3.
- Control style, pacing, and camera instructions.
- Compare multiple outputs from similar prompts.

---

## Demo Setup

Requirements:

- Access to Veo3.
- Understanding of structured prompting.
- Sample marketing scenarios.

---

## Instructor Walkthrough

### Step 1: Basic Prompt

The instructor will start with a simple prompt:
- Single scene.
- Minimal instructions.

Students will observe:
- Visual ambiguity.
- Generic motion.
- Lack of focus.

---

### Step 2: Structured Prompt

The instructor will refine the prompt to include:
- Scene description.
- Camera movement.
- Visual tone.
- Intended audience.

Students will compare results side by side.

---

### Step 3: Ad-Oriented Prompt

The instructor will demonstrate:
- Short runtime constraints.
- Product-focused framing.
- Clear visual storytelling.

Discussion:
- Why ads require different prompts than art.

---

## Demo Observations

- Video quality improves with specificity.
- Motion instructions matter.
- Short prompts waste model capacity.

---

## Demo Takeaways

- Text-to-video prompting is a skill.
- Structure beats creativity alone.
- Expect variation, not consistency.

## Code Along

# Veo3 — Text-to-Video Generation
## Imitate (Guided Do-Along)


---

## Goal

Students will create **AI-generated video ads** using Veo3.

---

## Project Overview

Each student will:

- Design a short video concept.
- Translate the concept into a structured prompt.
- Generate multiple video variations.
- Evaluate outputs for clarity and usefulness.

---

## Step 1: Concept Definition

Students will define:

- Product or service.
- Target audience.
- Core message.
- Desired emotion.

Instructor emphasis:
- Clarity before prompting.

---

## Step 2: Prompt Structure

Students will write prompts that include:

- Scene setting.
- Camera behavior.
- Motion pacing.
- Visual style.
- Runtime constraints.

Discussion:
- Why vague prompts fail.
- Why overloading prompts also fails.

---

## Step 3: Generate Video Variations

Students will:

- Generate at least 2 variations.
- Change one variable per iteration.
- Observe how outputs shift.

---

## Step 4: Review and Compare

Students will evaluate:
- Visual coherence.
- Message clarity.
- Suitability for ads.

Instructor checkpoints:
- Prompt intent alignment.
- Visual storytelling effectiveness.

---

## Imitation Takeaways

- Video prompting is design work.
- Iteration is mandatory.
- Expect usable, not perfect outputs.

## Practice Exercise

# Veo3 — Text-to-Video Generation
## Practice


---

## Independent Challenge

Students will create:

- One short video ad concept.
- Two different Veo3 prompts for the same concept.
- Two generated videos with different styles.

---

## Requirements

Each submission must include:

- The written prompt.
- A description of the intended outcome.
- Notes on what worked and what did not.

---

## Stretch Goals (Optional)

- Optimize for a specific platform.
- Create a prompt template for reuse.
- Experiment with pacing and camera language.

---

## Reflection Questions

Students should be able to answer:

- What prompt changes had the biggest impact?
- Where does Veo3 struggle?
- When should humans stay in the loop?
- How would this fit into a real ad pipeline?

---

## Practice Takeaways

- AI video is an accelerator, not a finish line.
- Prompting is creative direction.
- The value is speed, not perfection.
