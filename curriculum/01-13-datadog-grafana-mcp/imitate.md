# Datadog & Grafana MCP Servers
## Imitate (Guided Do-Along)

### Lesson Duration
45 minutes

---

## Goal

Students will perform **AI-assisted observability analysis** using Datadog and Grafana MCP servers.

---

## Exercise Overview

Students will:

- Connect to both MCP servers.
- Query telemetry data.
- Use AI to assist diagnosis.
- Validate findings manually.

---

## Step 1: Tool Familiarization

Students will:
- Review available MCP tools for each platform.
- Understand query capabilities.
- Identify strengths of each system.

---

## Step 2: Datadog Analysis

Students will:
- Query service health metrics.
- Identify anomalies.
- Extract high-level insights.

---

## Step 3: Grafana Analysis

Students will:
- Inspect time-series trends.
- Zoom into specific windows.
- Identify subtle performance issues.

---

## Step 4: Synthesis

Students will:
- Ask AI to combine findings.
- Propose a unified diagnosis.
- Validate against raw telemetry.

---

## Imitation Takeaways

- Correlation beats isolation.
- AI accelerates analysis.
- Context determines accuracy.
