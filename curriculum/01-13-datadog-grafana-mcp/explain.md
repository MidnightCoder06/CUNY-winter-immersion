# Datadog & Grafana MCP Servers
## Explain

### Lesson Duration
10–15 minutes

---

## Terminal Objectives (SWBAT)

By the end of this section, students will be able to:

- Explain the role of Datadog and Grafana in observability ecosystems.
- Describe how MCP servers expose third-party observability tools to AI systems.
- Compare Datadog and Grafana use cases.
- Identify scenarios where AI-assisted observability adds the most value.

---

## The Problem This Solves

Modern systems produce more telemetry than humans can manually interpret.

Dashboards help humans.  
MCP servers help **AI reason over telemetry**.

Datadog and Grafana are industry-standard tools.  
MCP makes them machine-accessible.

---

## Datadog Overview

Datadog is a full-stack observability platform.

It provides:
- Metrics
- Logs
- Traces
- Alerts
- Service maps

Datadog excels at **breadth and correlation**.

---

## Grafana Overview

Grafana focuses on visualization and querying.

It provides:
- Flexible dashboards
- Multi-source querying
- Strong time-series analysis

Grafana excels at **deep inspection and customization**.

---

## Why MCP Matters Here

MCP allows AI systems to:
- Query Datadog metrics programmatically.
- Inspect Grafana dashboards and data sources.
- Correlate signals across tools.
- Assist engineers during incidents.

This moves AI from “advisor” to **operator assistant**.

---

## Mental Model

Datadog and Grafana are sensory organs.  
MCP is the nervous system.  
AI is the reasoning layer.

---

## Key Takeaways

- MCP unlocks observability tools for AI.
- Datadog and Grafana serve different strengths.
- AI-assisted observability scales human attention.
