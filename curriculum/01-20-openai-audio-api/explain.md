# OpenAI Audio API
## Explain

### Lesson Duration
10–15 minutes

---

## Terminal Objectives (SWBAT)

By the end of this section, students will be able to:

- Explain what the OpenAI Audio API is.
- Describe the difference between text-to-speech and speech-to-text.
- Identify use cases for audio interfaces in AI systems.
- Explain tradeoffs between audio, text, and multimodal interfaces.

---

## Why Audio Matters

Audio is the most natural human interface.

Typing is optional.  
Speaking is instinctive.

Audio unlocks:
- Accessibility.
- Speed.
- Emotional context.
- Hands-free interaction.

---

## What Is the OpenAI Audio API?

The OpenAI Audio API enables:

- Text-to-speech (TTS).
- Speech-to-text (STT).
- Audio generation and processing.

It allows AI systems to **hear and speak**.

---

## Text-to-Speech (TTS)

TTS converts generated text into audio.

Use cases:
- Voice assistants.
- Audiobooks.
- Customer support responses.
- Accessibility tools.

Quality factors:
- Voice clarity.
- Tone.
- Latency.

---

## Speech-to-Text (STT)

STT converts spoken audio into text.

Use cases:
- Voice commands.
- Transcription.
- Meeting notes.
- Call analysis.

Accuracy depends on:
- Audio quality.
- Noise.
- Accents.
- Context.

---

## Why Audio Changes System Design

Audio systems must consider:
- Latency.
- Streaming vs batch.
- Error handling.
- User experience.

Mistakes feel more human when spoken.

---

## Mental Model

Text interfaces think in sentences.  
Audio interfaces think in **time**.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Audio increases reach and usability.
- Latency and accuracy matter.
- Audio systems require stronger UX discipline.
