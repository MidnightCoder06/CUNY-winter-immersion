# OpenAI Audio API
## Imitate (Guided Do-Along)

### Lesson Duration
45 minutes

---

## Goal

Students will build a **basic audio-enabled AI feature**.

---

## Project Overview

The feature will:

- Accept spoken input or text.
- Process it with AI.
- Return an audio response.

---

## Step 1: Interface Selection

Students will choose:
- Text-to-speech.
- Speech-to-text.
- Or both.

Instructor emphasis:
Start simple.

---

## Step 2: Audio Handling

Students will:
- Capture or load audio.
- Send audio to the API.
- Receive structured results.

---

## Step 3: Response Generation

Students will:
- Generate a text response.
- Convert it to speech.
- Play or store the audio.

---

## Step 4: UX Review

Students will:
- Evaluate response timing.
- Identify awkward phrasing.
- Improve clarity.

---

## Imitation Takeaways

- Audio amplifies strengths and weaknesses.
- Latency matters.
- Simplicity improves reliability.
