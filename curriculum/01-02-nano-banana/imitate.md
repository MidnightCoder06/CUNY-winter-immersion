# Nano Banana — Rapid Image Generation
## Imitate (Guided Do-Along)

### Lesson Duration
45 minutes

---

## Goal

Students will generate **visual concepts quickly** using Nano Banana.

---

## Project Overview

Each student will:

- Choose a product, service, or idea.
- Generate multiple visual concepts.
- Iterate rapidly based on outputs.
- Evaluate usefulness, not beauty.

---

## Step 1: Concept Selection

Students will define:

- The concept or product.
- The intended viewer.
- The purpose of the image.

Instructor emphasis:
- Purpose drives prompting.

---

## Step 2: Prompt Design

Students will write prompts that include:

- Primary subject.
- Environment or context.
- Style constraints.
- Intended usage (ad, slide, mockup).

Discussion:
- Why short prompts often work better here.

---

## Step 3: Rapid Iteration

Students will:

- Generate at least 3 image variations.
- Adjust one variable per iteration.
- Compare outputs for clarity and relevance.

---

## Step 4: Selection and Rationale

Students will choose:
- One image that best fits the goal.

They will explain:
- Why it works.
- Why others fail.

---

## Imitation Takeaways

- Speed changes decision-making.
- “Good enough” unlocks progress.
- This tool supports momentum, not polish.
