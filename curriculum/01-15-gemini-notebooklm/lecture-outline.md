# Gemini & NotebookLM — Workplace AI
## Explain


---

## Demo Objective

The instructor will demonstrate how to:

- Use Gemini inside workplace tools.
- Use NotebookLM for source-grounded research.
- Compare grounded vs ungrounded AI outputs.
- Identify appropriate use cases for each tool.

---

## Demo Setup

Requirements:

- Access to Gemini-enabled workplace tools.
- NotebookLM access.
- Sample documents and research materials.

---

## Instructor Walkthrough

### Step 1: Gemini in Documents

The instructor will:
- Draft a document using Gemini.
- Rewrite content with tone changes.
- Summarize long text inline.

Focus:
Speed and context awareness.

---

### Step 2: NotebookLM Research

The instructor will:
- Upload source documents.
- Ask questions grounded in those sources.
- Review citations.

Discussion:
Why grounding prevents hallucination.

---

### Step 3: Comparison

The instructor will:
- Ask the same question in both tools.
- Compare responses.
- Highlight strengths and weaknesses.

---

## Demo Observations

- Gemini accelerates creation.
- NotebookLM enforces discipline.
- Context controls reliability.

---

## Demo Takeaways

- Choose the right AI for the task.
- Grounding matters in professional settings.
- AI assists, humans decide.

# Gemini & NotebookLM — Workplace AI
## Imitate (Guided Do-Along)


---

## Goal

Students will use Gemini and NotebookLM to **complete a realistic workplace task**.

---

## Project Overview

Students will:

- Analyze a set of documents.
- Produce a summarized output.
- Draft a professional artifact.

---

## Step 1: Source Review

Students will:
- Review provided documents.
- Identify key themes.
- Note ambiguities.

Instructor emphasis:
Understand before generating.

---

## Step 2: NotebookLM Analysis

Students will:
- Upload documents to NotebookLM.
- Ask clarifying questions.
- Extract grounded insights.

---

## Step 3: Gemini Execution

Students will:
- Use Gemini to draft:
  - A summary.
  - An email.
  - A short report.

---

## Step 4: Review and Edit

Students will:
- Verify facts.
- Improve clarity.
- Adjust tone.

---

## Imitation Takeaways

- Research precedes writing.
- Grounding prevents errors.
- AI accelerates competent workflows.

## Practice


---

## Independent Challenge

Students will:

- Complete a workplace-style task using both tools.
- Document where each tool was used.
- Justify tool choices.

---

## Requirements

Each submission must include:

- Source materials.
- NotebookLM outputs.
- Gemini-generated drafts.
- Final edited version.

---

## Stretch Goals (Optional)

- Create a reusable workflow.
- Identify compliance risks.
- Propose team usage guidelines.

---

## Reflection Questions

Students should be able to answer:

- Where did grounding matter most?
- Where did Gemini save the most time?
- What risks remain?
- How should teams govern AI usage?

---

## Practice Takeaways

- AI is a collaborator.
- Context defines quality.
- Governance enables trust.
