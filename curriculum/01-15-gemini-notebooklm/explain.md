# Gemini & NotebookLM — Workplace AI
## Explain

### Lesson Duration
10–15 minutes

---

## Terminal Objectives (SWBAT)

By the end of this section, students will be able to:

- Explain how Gemini integrates into workplace productivity tools.
- Describe what NotebookLM is and how it differs from general chatbots.
- Identify knowledge-work tasks that benefit most from AI assistance.
- Explain the risks of using AI blindly in professional environments.

---

## The Shift in Knowledge Work

AI is moving from “tool you visit” to “coworker in your workflow.”

This changes:
- How documents are written.
- How research is done.
- How decisions are supported.

The goal is not replacement.  
The goal is **cognitive leverage**.

---

## What Is Gemini in the Workplace?

Gemini integrates directly into tools like:
- Docs
- Sheets
- Slides
- Email

It assists with:
- Drafting
- Summarizing
- Rewriting
- Analyzing content in place

Gemini works where work already happens.

---

## What Is NotebookLM?

NotebookLM is an AI-powered research and synthesis tool.

It works by:
- Ingesting your source materials.
- Answering questions grounded only in those sources.
- Citing where information came from.

NotebookLM is **context-bound**, not free-ranging.

---

## Why These Tools Matter Together

Gemini helps you *produce*.  
NotebookLM helps you *think*.

Together they:
- Reduce cognitive load.
- Improve consistency.
- Speed up analysis and communication.

---

## Common Use Cases

- Meeting notes and summaries.
- Research synthesis.
- Internal documentation.
- Strategy drafts.
- Knowledge transfer.

---

## Mental Model

Gemini is an **executor**.  
NotebookLM is a **research assistant**.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Embedded AI changes workflows.
- Source grounding matters.
- Judgment remains human.
