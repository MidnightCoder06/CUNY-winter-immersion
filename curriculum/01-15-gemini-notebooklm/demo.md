# Gemini & NotebookLM — Workplace AI
## Demonstrate

### Lesson Duration
15 minutes

---

## Demo Objective

The instructor will demonstrate how to:

- Use Gemini inside workplace tools.
- Use NotebookLM for source-grounded research.
- Compare grounded vs ungrounded AI outputs.
- Identify appropriate use cases for each tool.

---

## Demo Setup

Requirements:

- Access to Gemini-enabled workplace tools.
- NotebookLM access.
- Sample documents and research materials.

---

## Instructor Walkthrough

### Step 1: Gemini in Documents

The instructor will:
- Draft a document using Gemini.
- Rewrite content with tone changes.
- Summarize long text inline.

Focus:
Speed and context awareness.

---

### Step 2: NotebookLM Research

The instructor will:
- Upload source documents.
- Ask questions grounded in those sources.
- Review citations.

Discussion:
Why grounding prevents hallucination.

---

### Step 3: Comparison

The instructor will:
- Ask the same question in both tools.
- Compare responses.
- Highlight strengths and weaknesses.

---

## Demo Observations

- Gemini accelerates creation.
- NotebookLM enforces discipline.
- Context controls reliability.

---

## Demo Takeaways

- Choose the right AI for the task.
- Grounding matters in professional settings.
- AI assists, humans decide.
