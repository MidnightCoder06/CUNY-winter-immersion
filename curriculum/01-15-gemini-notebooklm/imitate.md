# Gemini & NotebookLM — Workplace AI
## Imitate (Guided Do-Along)

### Lesson Duration
45 minutes

---

## Goal

Students will use Gemini and NotebookLM to **complete a realistic workplace task**.

---

## Project Overview

Students will:

- Analyze a set of documents.
- Produce a summarized output.
- Draft a professional artifact.

---

## Step 1: Source Review

Students will:
- Review provided documents.
- Identify key themes.
- Note ambiguities.

Instructor emphasis:
Understand before generating.

---

## Step 2: NotebookLM Analysis

Students will:
- Upload documents to NotebookLM.
- Ask clarifying questions.
- Extract grounded insights.

---

## Step 3: Gemini Execution

Students will:
- Use Gemini to draft:
  - A summary.
  - An email.
  - A short report.

---

## Step 4: Review and Edit

Students will:
- Verify facts.
- Improve clarity.
- Adjust tone.

---

## Imitation Takeaways

- Research precedes writing.
- Grounding prevents errors.
- AI accelerates competent workflows.
