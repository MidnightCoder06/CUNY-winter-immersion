# Workflow Automation — n8n & ComfyUI
## Practice

### Lesson Duration
15 minutes

---

## Independent Challenge

Students will:

- Design an automated AI workflow.
- Document each step.
- Explain the business value.

---

## Requirements

Each submission must include:

- Workflow diagram or description.
- Trigger definition.
- AI processing step.
- Final output.

---

## Stretch Goals (Optional)

- Add error handling.
- Add logging or alerts.
- Chain multiple AI steps.

---

## Reflection Questions

Students should be able to answer:

- What part benefits most from automation?
- Where could this fail?
- How does automation change team behavior?
- What would break at scale?

---

## Practice Takeaways

- Automation multiplies effort.
- Systems create consistency.
- Manual work does not scale.
