# Workflow Automation — n8n & ComfyUI
## Explain

### Lesson Duration
10–15 minutes

---

## Terminal Objectives (SWBAT)

By the end of this section, students will be able to:

- Explain what workflow automation is and why it matters.
- Describe how n8n enables event-driven automation.
- Explain what ComfyUI is and how it fits into AI pipelines.
- Identify use cases where automation compounds value.

---

## What Is Workflow Automation?

Workflow automation connects tools and processes so work happens without manual intervention.

Think of automation as **installing leverage**.  
You do the work once.  
The system does it forever.

---

## What Is n8n?

n8n is a low-code workflow automation platform.

It allows you to:
- React to triggers.
- Transform data.
- Call APIs.
- Chain tools together.

n8n is logic glue.

---

## What Is ComfyUI?

ComfyUI is a node-based interface for building AI generation pipelines.

It is commonly used for:
- Image generation.
- Video generation.
- Model experimentation.
- Complex AI workflows.

ComfyUI is visual orchestration for AI.

---

## Why Combine n8n and ComfyUI?

Together they allow:
- Automated AI content creation.
- Trigger-based generation.
- Scalable creative pipelines.

This moves AI from **tool** to **system**.

---

## Common Use Cases

- Automated content pipelines.
- Lead intake → AI processing → CRM.
- Scheduled asset generation.
- Multi-step AI workflows.

---

## Mental Model

n8n decides *when* and *why*.  
ComfyUI decides *how*.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Automation compounds effort.
- n8n orchestrates logic.
- ComfyUI orchestrates AI.
- Systems beat manual execution.
