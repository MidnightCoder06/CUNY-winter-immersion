## The Core Problem This Solves

Large language models do not remember your data by default.  
They respond based on training, not your documents.

Embeddings solve this by turning text into numbers that represent meaning.

Think of embeddings as **coordinates for ideas**.

---

## What Are Embeddings?

Embeddings are numerical representations of text.  
Similar meanings produce vectors that are close together.

This allows systems to:
- Compare meaning instead of keywords.
- Search by intent.
- Retrieve relevant context dynamically.

---

## What Are Files?

Files allow you to upload raw content such as:
- PDFs
- Text documents
- Notes
- Logs

They are the **source material**.
